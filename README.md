## 1. The Problem

Hearing loss is a major disability, but high-quality hearing aids can be expensive. Understanding the fundamental analog electronics behind them is the first step to creating more accessible devices. The goal was to design a simple, low-cost circuit to amplify audio signals from a microphone for a speaker.

## 2. Our Solution

For our "Analog Electronics" final project, our team (Kelompok 10) designed and simulated a complete **analog hearing aid circuit**.

The circuit consists of several stages:
1.  **Pre-Amplifier:** Uses a JFET to capture the small signal from a microphone.
2.  **Amplifier:** Uses an Op-Amp to significantly boost the audio signal.
3.  **Power Amplifier:** A final BJT (push-pull) stage to provide enough power to drive a small speaker.
4.  **MOSFET Switch:** An Arduino-controlled switch was included in the design.

The entire circuit was designed, tested, and simulated in **Proteus**.
